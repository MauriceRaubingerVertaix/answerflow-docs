Runs on Atlassian · Zero External Data Egress

Privacy Policy & Data Security Architecture

Effective Date: November 25, 2026 · Vendor: Vortaix Labs

1. Zero External Egress Guarantee

AnswerFlow for Confluence is engineered strictly on Atlassian's serverless Forge platform and is designed to qualify for the Runs on Atlassian program:

  • Zero External Servers: AnswerFlow operates no external vendor servers, databases, or third-party cloud infrastructure.
  • Zero Data Egress: AnswerFlow declares zero external network egress permissions in its manifest. No Confluence content, questions, answers, or user details ever leave Atlassian's cloud trust boundary.
  • Zero-Egress AI: Optional AI Answer Deflection executes exclusively via Atlassian's native Forge LLMs API (@forge/llm) inside Atlassian's managed boundary. Administrators can disable AI Answer Deflection or adjust the daily quota at any time.
  • Zero Third-Party Analytics or Trackers: AnswerFlow does not embed third-party tracking scripts, advertising SDKs, or external telemetry services.

2. Data Stored & Data Residency

All app data—including questions, answers, votes, tags, space RBAC configurations, and moderation audit entries—is stored exclusively inside Atlassian Forge Storage (@forge/kvs) pinned to your Confluence Cloud instance's configured Atlassian Data Residency region.

3. Permission & Access Isolation

When running CQL searches for AI Answer Deflection or creating promoted documentation pages, AnswerFlow invokes Confluence REST APIs strictly using asUser(). This guarantees Confluence's native space and page restrictions are enforced at all times—users can never retrieve content from restricted pages they lack permission to view.

4. Data Retention, Export & Deletion

  • Portability: Site administrators and moderators can export all questions, answers, tags, and statuses in JSON or CSV format at any time via the Governance & Migration tab.
  • Deletion: Uninstalling AnswerFlow from your Confluence Cloud instance follows Atlassian's standard Forge app data lifecycle and deletion policies.
  • GDPR & Data Subject Requests: Because Vortaix Labs never receives or stores customer content outside your Atlassian tenant, your Confluence administrator retains direct control over all data. For assistance, contact raubingermaurice@gmail.com.

5. Sub-processors

AnswerFlow uses zero external vendor sub-processors. All compute, storage, and AI inference are provided exclusively by Atlassian Pty Ltd under your existing Atlassian Cloud customer agreement.

6. Contact

Security inquiries, vulnerability disclosures, or privacy questions may be directed to Vortaix Labs at raubingermaurice@gmail.com.
